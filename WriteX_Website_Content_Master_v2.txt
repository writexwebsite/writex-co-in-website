META DESCRIPTION:
Read the WriteX terms of service for academic support, research guidance, editing, formatting, referencing, quote requests, payments, and responsible use.

H1:
Terms of Service

Intro:
These terms explain how WriteX provides academic support, research guidance, editing, formatting, referencing, originality review, SOP/admissions support, and related services.

Sections:
1. Nature of service
WriteX provides academic support, research guidance, editing, proofreading, formatting, originality review, referencing support, admissions support, and model solutions for learning purposes.

2. Student responsibility
Students are responsible for following their institution's academic integrity policies. WriteX support should be used for learning, guidance, drafting support, editing, review, and document improvement.

3. Quotes and scope
Quotes depend on service type, academic level, subject, word count, deadline, file condition, and review depth. Scope must be confirmed before work begins.

4. Files and instructions
Clients should provide accurate briefs, rubrics, drafts, supervisor comments, deadlines, and formatting requirements. New instructions may affect scope, timeline, and pricing.

5. Payment
Payment terms depend on the invoice, agreed scope, and support arrangement. Final download access may be subject to payment settlement where applicable.

6. Revisions
Revision support depends on the original brief, agreed scope, and supplied instructions. New requirements may be treated as additional scope.

7. Confidentiality
WriteX does not publicly display student files, identities, or private academic documents.

8. Limitations
WriteX does not guarantee grades, marks, admission outcomes, or institutional decisions.

9. Contact
For questions, contact WriteX through the official website communication channels.
