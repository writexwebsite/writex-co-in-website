# AXO Service Rules

The source of truth is `lib/axo/config.ts`. Public categories are limited to Coursework & Brief Support, Dissertation & Thesis Support, SOP & Admissions Support, Academic Editing & Proofreading, Originality & AI Review, and Formatting & Referencing Support.

All requests use manual scope review. AXO never calculates price, promises feasibility, grades, detector results, plagiarism percentages, turnaround, refund rights or revision entitlement. Complex dissertation and urgent work must be escalated to the team.
